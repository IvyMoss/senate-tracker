HOW TO READ THIS TRACKER

Sources and methodology

Each race links to the source of the displayed count. Among comparable feeds, the dashboard uses the highest total votes, then the most recent race update if totals tie. Verified certified results take precedence. The dashboard checks for published results every 30 seconds while open.

The balance starts from the 47–53 split specified for this tracker. It sets aside the nine tracked seats and assumes other seats follow their starting alignment. Only certified outcomes from tracked races enter the results-based tally. A reported lead, runoff, or incomplete ranked-choice tabulation leaves a seat unresolved.

Vote shares use all reported candidate votes, including write-ins when supplied. Reporting progress counts reporting units. A 50–50 Senate leaves the vice president with the tie-breaking vote.
